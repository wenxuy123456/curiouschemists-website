Example Sessions

Each school partnership is shaped around its students. These are a few of the experiments we may bring across recurring visits.

01

Elephant Toothpaste

Watch a rapid chemical reaction create a towering stream of colorful foam while learning about catalysts and decomposition.

02

Instant Snow

Explore polymers and absorption by transforming a tiny amount of powder into handfuls of fluffy “snow.”

03

Lava Lamp

Combine colorful liquids and watch bubbles rise and fall while exploring density, polarity, and chemical reactions.

04

Catapult Competition

Build and test a craft-stick catapult, then compete for distance and accuracy while learning about force, energy, and motion.
