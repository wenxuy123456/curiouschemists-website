Our story

How CuriousChemists Began

CuriousChemists started with a simple observation: too many elementary students never get to experience the excitement of real science.

Textbook learning is valuable, but watching a foam volcano erupt? That’s a memory that sticks. Founded by a high school junior, the program grew from a single classroom visit into a structured STEM outreach initiative spanning multiple schools and sessions.

Built by students,
for students.
“The goal isn’t just to teach, it’s to show every student that they belong in science.”
Our Mission

To inspire young students with fun, hands-on STEM activities & incorporate basic AI concepts to spread digital literacy.
